What I do

I write synth-driven music with fantastical, science-fiction vibes. Usually, the harmonic textures and fleeting melodies lead to atmospheres that sit under the scene, but my music also artfully peeks above the surface when the moment is right.

GamesScores, adaptive loops, and menu / event cues for indie and studio projects
TabletopAmbient beds and scene music for TTRPG sessions and actual-play
SyncLicensing for film, trailers, and media that's cleared and ready to drop in
CustomOriginal commissions written to your brief, with stems on request
